Tutorial

Step 1 / 5
🏠

Browse the template library

On the left sidebar, you can filter templates by style (light, dark, minimal) or by role (tech, design, finance, etc.).

Click any filter button and the gallery will show only matching resume templates. Each card includes a live iframe thumbnail so you can preview the layout quickly.

Tip: click the dark theme filter to explore more futuristic layouts.
🖱️

Choose and open a resume

Once you find a template you like, click the card to open the editor page.

The page opens in preview mode, which matches the final print layout. Your data is stored in your browser and is never uploaded to a server.

Tip: you can return to the home page anytime and try another template. Each template saves data separately.
✏️

Edit resume content

After opening a resume, the floating toolbar appears in the lower-right corner. Click the ✏️ Edit button (or press Ctrl+E) to enter edit mode.

In edit mode, all editable text shows a blue dashed outline. Click directly to modify it. Experience and skill blocks also include [+] [×] buttons for adding or removing items.

The avatar area shows a 📷 upload prompt when hovered; you can drag and crop it after uploading.

Tip: your edits are auto-saved to browser storage so refreshing the page won’t lose your work.
🖨️

Print and export

When editing is complete, click the 🖨️ Print button (or press Ctrl+P) to open the system print dialog.

All templates are optimized for A4 paper, and the toolbar and editing guides are hidden automatically during printing. Dark-theme color schemes are preserved.

In the print dialog, choose Save as PDF to export a PDF document.

Tip: click the 👁️ button in edit mode to return to preview mode and review the final layout.
🎨
